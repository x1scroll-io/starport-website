Privacy Policy

Starport LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company · Effective October 2, 2026

Who we are

Starport ("we", "us") is operated by Starport LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, reachable at support@starportwallet.xyz. Privacy requests should use the subject line "Privacy request". Product website: starportwallet.app.

What Starport is

Starport is a self-custodial, cross-chain wallet extension. It lets you sign one transaction per blockchain family and routes the journey through bridge providers (LI.FI, XDEX, Jupiter, Warp, CCTP, ChangeNow, THORChain).

What we do NOT collect

Your seed phrase, private keys, or passphrase. These are generated and stored locally on your device, encrypted. We never see them, and they are never transmitted to us.

Your full transaction contents beyond what's needed to route a swap. Approval details are shown to you in the popup before anything is signed.

What we DO process (and why)

  • Wallet addresses and transaction hashes — to route swaps through the bridge providers and to display your activity.
  • Device/extension metadata (version, network) — to keep the extension working and diagnose faults.
  • Bridge-provider data — a quote/swap is sent to the provider (e.g. ChangeNow, LI.FI) to execute the route you approved. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Third-party providers

Starport integrates third parties. When you use a route, the necessary transaction data goes to that provider to complete your swap. Their handling is governed by their own policies: ChangeNow, LI.FI/Jumper, Jupiter, THORChain, XDEX.

Data retention

Starport is non-custodial and does not operate a wallet server. We do not retain your seed phrase, private keys, or passphrase under any circumstances, and we keep no server-side logs of private-key material or seed phrases. Transaction data is processed transiently to complete and display a route. Where a bridge provider completes a route, that provider's own retention policy applies to the data it receives.

Security

Your keys are encrypted at rest on your device. We recommend using a strong password and keeping your recovery phrase offline.

Your rights & contact

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. To exercise any data rights or ask questions, contact support@starportwallet.xyz (subject: "Privacy request").

Changes

We will post changes to this policy here and update the "Effective" date.
